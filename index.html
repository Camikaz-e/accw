Who We Are

PRECISION CONCRETE ENGINEERING

Arusha Concrete and Cement Works Limited (ACCW) manufactures high-performance prestressed spun concrete poles built for East Africa's toughest infrastructure demands. Our centrifugal casting process produces dense, uniform poles with compressive strengths exceeding C50 — engineered to last 50+ years in the harshest environments.

// CAST

Centrifugal Spinning

High-speed mould rotation removes water and voids, producing uniformly dense concrete walls.

// CURE

Steam Curing

Controlled heat and humidity ensure consistent strength gain throughout every pole section.

// TEST

Load Certification

Every batch is destructively and non-destructively tested to IEC 60068 and TCPM standards.

// WIRE

HT Prestress Steel

High-tensile wires pre-tensioned to 70% UTS deliver superior flexural crack resistance.

FACTORY PRODUCTION YARD
Manufacturing Process

FROM WIRE TO STANDING POLE

A six-stage controlled production process turns raw steel and concrete into infrastructure that lasts 50+ years.

01
Wire Tensioning

HT steel wires are mechanically prestressed in the mould bed to 70% of their UTS before any concrete is poured.

02
Concrete Batching

Low w/c ratio mix is computer-batched from certified aggregate, cement, and admixtures for target grade.

03
Centrifugal Casting

Mould spins at multi-speed program to uniformly distribute and compact concrete around the prestress cage.

04
Steam Curing

Controlled temperature-humidity cycle accelerates strength gain and ensures consistent curing depth.

05
QC Inspection

Dimensional checks, visual inspection, cover measurement, and marking before demoulding release.

06
Load Testing

Destructive and non-destructive bending tests to IEC/TCPM — certificated and logged per batch.
