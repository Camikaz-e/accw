Our Full Range

MORE PRODUCTS WE PRODUCE

Beyond our prestressed concrete poles, ACCW manufactures a full range of concrete building and infrastructure products — concrete blocks (tofali), concrete fencing poles, and concrete bricks — all produced to the same rigorous quality standard.

CONCRETE BLOCKS · TOFALI
Product 01

CONCRETE BLOCKS (TOFALI)

Our concrete blocks, known locally as tofali, are manufactured to consistent dimensional tolerances and compressive strength for reliable load-bearing and partition walling. Produced in standard sizes for residential, commercial, and industrial construction across Arusha and Tanzania.

Available in solid and hollow block formats, cured under controlled conditions to ensure uniform strength and reduced shrinkage cracking. Bulk orders and site delivery available throughout Tanzania.

Product 02

CONCRETE FENCING POLES

Precast concrete fencing poles engineered for durability and long-term weather resistance, ideal for perimeter walls, boundary demarcation, and security fencing on residential, agricultural, and commercial properties.

Manufactured with reinforced concrete for structural rigidity, resistant to termites, rot, and corrosion — a low-maintenance alternative to timber or steel fencing posts. Available in multiple heights to match your fencing specification.

CONCRETE PAVING BRICKS
Product 03

CONCRETE BRICKS (PAVING)

Durable concrete paving bricks designed for outdoor flooring — driveways, walkways, courtyards, and compound paving. Manufactured for high compressive strength and slip resistance to withstand vehicle and foot traffic in all weather conditions.

Interlocking and standard formats available, with consistent sizing for clean, professional-looking paved surfaces. Bulk supply available for residential and commercial projects across Tanzania.
